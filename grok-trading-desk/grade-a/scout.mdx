## What it is

`scripts/scout_score.py`. Pure scoring. Raw V1.4 component caps sum to **74**, so raw Scout cannot naturally cross Grade A. Grade A reachability is a Champion-layer bridge, not a lowered threshold.

`is_grade_b(score)` is 45–74 inclusive, exclusive of 75.

## Clock and size

Runs inside drain per candidate. No timer of its own.

## Radio

Scout does not publish. `record_grade_b_shadow(..., telegram=False)` is tracking, not a card.

## Host

Inside `grok-desk-drain`. Historical baseline component refreshes every 6h via `grok-desk-baselines.timer` → `scripts/build_predictive_baselines.py`.

## State / settle

Scores persist with the candidate. Grade B shadow tracking stays in `grok_desk.grade_b_shadow`.

## Gates

- Thresholds: B 45–74, A 75+, A+ 85+
- Market context is advisory and is **not** a scoring input
- Missing / UNKNOWN historical cannot qualify for the Grade A bridge

## How Patrick uses it

A 74 print is still Grade B until Champion applies the confluence bridge. Do not treat raw Scout 74 as Grade A.

## Decisions that still bind

Do not raise component caps or add an arbitrary bonus — that would lift ordinary Grade B without evidence. See [Grade A decisions](/grok-trading-desk/grade-a/decisions).
