## What it is

`scripts/grade_a_lane.py` plus `matching_setup_families` in `scripts/setup_families.py`. Grade B still classifies **one** exclusive family per symbol. Grade A then scores **every** evidence-backed paper family on that same event bundle.

Admitted on Grade A:

- `RELATIVE_STRENGTH_ROTATION`
- `REDUMP_CONTINUATION_SHORT`
- `FUNDING_DISLOCATION`

Blocked on Grade A (stay on their own books):

- `TREND_CONTINUATION` / `RECLAIM_CONTINUATION` / `EXTREME_MOMENTUM` — Expandables
- `BREAKOUT_CONFIRMATION` — sat-box / Hunter, not this paper book

A measured 5m UP tape that would have classified as TREND used to eat the RS / VOL / FUNDING seats. Grade A now skips Expandables and still evaluates the paper families.
