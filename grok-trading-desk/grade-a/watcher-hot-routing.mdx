## What it is

`watcher.py` (host `watcher_shared.py`) is the deterministic Bitget event detector. It consumes shared socket/bars. It does **not** own a Bitget WebSocket.

A USDT-M symbol becomes a Scout candidate only when the drain window has at least one watcher event in `PRICE`, `VOLUME`, `VOLATILITY`, `DERIVATIVES`, `LIQUIDITY`, or `RELATIVE`.

## Clock and size

Always-on `grok-desk-watcher.service`. Debounce 600s. HOT is 40 + runners. `EVENT_RULESET_V1` unchanged.

## Radio

Watcher does not Telegram. Ordinary ticks / HOT-only / ARMED ticks do not re-open Scout.

## Host

Consumer only. Hydrates shared 1m bars. `CONTEXT_ANCHORS` = `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT` may be quoted for health. They are not a candidate gate.

## State / settle

Events persist to `grok_desk.market_events`.

## Gates

- HOT list membership alone is not eligibility
- Zero-event symbols do not get Scout
- All families/directions/event_ids for one symbol coalesce into **one** candidate per drain
- Weekend sat-box `ARMED_SYMS` / `ARMED_BOOK` unchanged
