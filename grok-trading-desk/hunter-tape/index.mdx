Hunter Tape is the always-on packer book. It is cheap structure on shared 5m bars. It is **not** the brain product.

## What it is

`scripts/hunter_ripe_watch.py` scans GRAIN_PULLBACK, SWEEP_RECLAIM, BREAKOUT, EXTREME_MOMENTUM, and EARLY_* . Writes ripe state. May POST `ripe_changed`. Telegram TAPE is optional and muted by default.

## Clock and size

60s ripe timer. Extreme path on `market_context.json` change. 5m bars from Valkey / `bars_5m.json`.

## Radio

Lane `HUNTER`, muted by default. TAPE cards are shortlist. Brain TAKEs live under [Hunter Unique](/grok-trading-desk/hunter-unique).

## Host

`grok-hunter-ripe.timer`. Lock: `/var/lib/grok-desk/hunter-ripe.lock`. Lean host copy may exist under `deploy/host/` — canonical watcher is this repo's script.
