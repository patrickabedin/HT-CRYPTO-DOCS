## What it is

`scripts/grade_b_portfolio.py` and `scripts/grade_b_book_lock.py` bound how many Grade B names can be open at once. Discovery stays broad. Admission is ranked and capped.

Policy name: `GRADE_B_PORTFOLIO_V1`. `MAX_OPEN = 10`.

## Clock and size

Admission runs on the armed-watcher cycle inside the shared lock. Paper exits:

- Stop or TP1 at an observed valid price
- Two-hour holding limit
- 30-minute stall if the trade never reached +0.25R
- 50% giveback after reaching +1R

Quote outages delay settlement. They do not invent a fill.

Book lock (whole open book, not one ticket):

- Arm `GRADE_B_BOOK_LOCK_USDT` default **\$150**
- **Mission:** capture the UTC-day **fill-true open-book high**, flatten **every** open seat, sit out until the next UTC day. The Grade B calendar cell is that locked high (or locked loss), not Σ ticket closes.
- Once armed, a ticket **TP1** or profit giveback flattens the **whole** book as `BOOK_LOCK` at the current mark and pauses the UTC day. Do not let one ticket peel the winner and dump leftovers.
- Giveback `GRADE_B_BOOK_LOCK_GIVEBACK` default **25%** of the **fill-true UTC-day peak** is the backup if nothing hits TP.
- `fill_true_high_usdt` / `book_peak_usdt` trails fill × Bitget mark when attached, else fill × WS last
- `book_high_usdt` may still show a prefill last-chase high for display (`prefill_high_usdt`)
- `clear_prefill_lock()` keeps the display high and resets the lock peak to 0 so a prefill `BOOK_GIVEBACK` does not keep the day paused

## Radio

Measured pre-selection candidates do not publish entry cards. Selected measured alerts use the frozen plan. Book HIGH cards are a separate publisher (`grade_b_upl_high`) — see [Fills, levels, and UPL](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/fills-levels-upl).

## Host

Same process as the armed watcher. No extra timer. State: `/var/lib/grok-desk/grade_b_portfolio.json` and `.pause.json`. Do not delete them during a rollout — the journal prevents replay.

## State / settle

Paper closes persist to ClickHouse and the portfolio journal. Demo reconciliation follows acknowledged paper closes, never an empty or stale cache.

Tape fields: UTC-day high, low before that high, path low, max concurrent, fill-true high, prefill high. `book_samples` is a count, not a tick list.

## Gates

- At most **ten** candidate positions across open Grade B paper **and** tracked/unmatched demo
- One position per coin
- At most three in one direction and two per engine
- Rank by existing score, then estimated after-fee TP1 reward/risk, then earlier arm timestamp and stable ID
- Stale or missing demo state blocks new paper admission when demo trading is enabled
- An old unlimited `BITGET_DEMO_MAX_OPEN` cannot bypass the seat cap
- Flatten pause (`pause.json` `until`) blocks new takes; `flatten_cooldown` is silent
