## What it is

Grade B setup family `REDUMP_CONTINUATION_SHORT`. A dump day (`chg24 ≤ −4%`) that already bounced off the first 24h low, then fails mid-range and re-breaks that low. First-flush / grind at the lows is not the trade.

Helpers: `is_dump_tape` and `redump_continuation_ok` in `scripts/setup_families.py`. ARM builder: `build_redump_continuation_short` in `scripts/arm_gate.py`.

## Clock and size

24h range and `pos24`. Trigger is a break of the 24h low. Last must still be above that low when the book arms. Grade B demo size is unchanged when TRIGGERED.

## Radio

Grade B card through the existing ARMED + ALIGNED chief gates. `TELEGRAM_ALLOW` default includes `GRADE_B`. No new publisher. Do not test-send Telegram.

## Host

Runs inside drain. No dedicated timer. Do not restart `grok-market-data` for this tune.

## State / settle

Same Grade B paper and demo book. Live TP/SL, `fill_entry`, marks, flatten, and smoke are not part of this tune.

## Gates

Classify **and** ARM only a failed bounce:

- Dump day: `chg24 ≤ −4%` (`CHG24_STRONG`)
- `pos24` **0.30–0.50** (`POS24_REDUMP_MIN` / `POS24_REDUMP_MAX`)
- `VOLUME` + `PRICE`, one-sided down
- Last still above the 24h low

Loose dump tape that fails quality is `NO_SUPPORTED_SETUP`. It does **not** fall through to EARLY or RS as a consolation short. `redump_structure` False or UNKNOWN fails ARM.

## How Patrick uses it

Read the family on the card. A coin that is already sitting on the 24h low is not this trade. If he pastes a card, that is a [trade-check](/grok-trading-desk/advisory/trade-check).

## Pitfalls

- Do not short the first flush (`pos24` near the 24h low).
- Do not treat presets or empty `holdSide` as protected. See [Open + TP/SL](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/bitget-open-tpsl).
- Do not leak a failed dump into EARLY or RS.
- Do not remake attached books from public last.
