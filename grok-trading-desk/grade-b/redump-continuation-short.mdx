## What it is

A **SHORT** continuation after a dump already happened, price **bounced and failed to reclaim the 24h mid**, and selling resumes toward a break of the 24h low.

It is not "the coin is red, short the next low." That first-flush print is the bleed.

Code: `scripts/setup_families.py` (`redump_continuation_ok`) and `scripts/arm_gate.py` (`build_redump_continuation_short`).

## Gates

Must all be true to classify **and** ARM:

1. 24h change ≤ −4% (`CHG24_STRONG`).
2. `pos24` in **0.30–0.50** — still off the low, still below mid (failed bounce).
3. Watcher `VOLUME` + `PRICE` and **one-sided `down`**. Two-sided tape is not a redump.
4. Measured 24h box present. Last still **above** the 24h low (entry not already gone).

Loose dump tape (`chg24` ≤ −4% + VOLUME + down) that **fails** those quality checks is `NO_SUPPORTED_SETUP`. It does **not** fall through to `EARLY_IGNITION` or `RELATIVE_STRENGTH_ROTATION` as a consolation short.

Missing `pos24` or `chg24` → no redump (UNKNOWN, do not invent).
