Demo cash is the only book that can repay the $15k loan. Target: bank **+$50** and sit the UTC day, or cut at **−$50** and sit. One shared wallet. Three named engines. Nothing else gets a new Bitget fill.

$15k / $50 ≈ **300 perfect green days**. This desk cannot promise that. It *can* stop another −$650 day on the account that matters.

SHADOW / advisory only. No live orders. No Telegram publisher. Grade A stays 75.

## Who sits

| Engine | Why |
|---|---|
| `TREND_CONTINUATION` | Only Grade B family with real +$50 paper days |
| `RECLAIM_CONTINUATION` | Thin, already printed a +$50 day |
| `EXTREME_MOMENTUM` | Hunter tape; one seat if ripe is actionable. Empty seat is fine |

Hard reject everything else (REDUMP, RS, BREAKOUT, EARLY*, SCALP, CLIP GO). One coin and one engine per seat. **Max 3 open.**

Admission is mutually exclusive with the [Grade B 10-seat book](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/portfolio-book-lock). A TREND cannot sit in both.

## Locks (cash, not tape high)

Combined Expandables cash = UTC-day realized $ + open-book uPnL:

- **Bank:** cash ≥ **+$50** → flatten all, sit until next UTC day. Reason `EXPAND_BANK`. Giveback **0** (hard first touch).
- **Cut:** cash ≤ **−$50** → flatten all, sit. Reason `EXPAND_STOP`.

Do **not** reuse `GRADE_B_BOOK_LOCK_USDT=150` on this process. State is `EXPAND_PORTFOLIO_STATE` (`/var/lib/grok-desk/expand_portfolio.json`). Pause is the sibling `.pause.json`.

Calendar cell $ is banked ticket cash (same rule as Grade B now). Tape High stays. Stamp `EXPANDABLES_PORTFOLIO_V1` on settles.
