## What it is

Grade B setup family `RELATIVE_STRENGTH_ROTATION`. An alt is stronger or weaker than **both** BTC and ETH while BTC itself is moving, and the trade goes **with** that BTC tape. A lone `RELATIVE` watcher tick is not a rotation.

Helper: `relative_rotation_ok` in `scripts/setup_families.py`. ARM builder: `build_relative_strength_rotation` in `scripts/arm_gate.py`.

## Clock and size

24h quote RS versus live `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT` fractional change. Trigger is a 24h high (LONG) or low (SHORT) break. Grade B demo size is unchanged when TRIGGERED. This is not a Hunter 5m engine.

## Host

## State / settle

Same Grade B paper and demo book. Episode key is symbol + family + direction + UTC date. Live TP/SL, `fill_entry`, marks, flatten, and smoke are not part of this tune.

## Gates

Classifier:

- `RELATIVE` **and** `PRICE` **and** one-sided
- LONG: `pos24 >= 0.55` (`POS24_RS_LONG_MIN`)
- SHORT: `pos24 <= 0.45` (`POS24_RS_SHORT_MAX`)
- Lone `RELATIVE` → `NO_SUPPORTED_SETUP`
- Mid-box RELATIVE plus PRICE → `NO_SUPPORTED_SETUP`

ARM (`relative_rotation_ok`):

- Both `rs_vs_btc` **and** `rs_vs_eth` agree (AND, not OR)
- BTC 24h move at least 1% (`RS_BTC_MOVE_MIN`)
- LONG only if BTC is at least +1%; SHORT only if BTC is at most −1%
- Same `pos24` band as the classifier
- Missing BTC/ETH or quote → `rs_supports_direction=UNKNOWN` → do not ARM
- Quality false → do not ARM

## How Patrick uses it

## Pitfalls

- Do not treat a `RELATIVE` watcher tick as RS.
- Do not OR `rs_vs_btc` / `rs_vs_eth`. Both legs must agree.
- Do not ARM when BTC is flat (under 1% 24h). That slice printed −42R.
- Do not fade BTC on this family.
- Do not remake attached paper and demo from public last so the columns match. See [Open + TP/SL](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/bitget-open-tpsl).
