Grade B is a **score band plus a book**, not a hunter-style named engine. Score **45–74**. Sixteen GROK_NATIVE setup families. This is the default Telegram radio.

<Warning>
  SHADOW / advisory. Bitget demo paper is opt-in only. Live keys stay read-only. Grade A stays 75 — do not lower it to grow this book.
</Warning>

## What it is

Scout and Champion score a candidate. If the score is 45–74 and ARM produced a measured thesis, the armed watcher waits for the trigger on a **completed** candle. TRIGGERED freezes the plan. Paper and (when enabled) Bitget demo copy that freeze.

Cap: **one coin**, **ten** open seats. Refill when a seat frees. Book lock arms at **\$150** and trails **25%** of the fill-true UTC-day peak.

Attached paper and demo Last/uPnL follow the Bitget demo mark, not public last. **Next agent:** before changing fills, marks, demo opens, TP/SL, flatten, or smoke, read [Open + TP/SL pitfalls](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/bitget-open-tpsl) so we do not repeat 13 Sep. Day notes: [13 Sep 2026](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/2026-09-13-upl-honesty).

## Clock and size

- Armed checks run on completed candles (15m close-through-level where that is the trigger).
- Demo / paper size: **USD 50 margin at 20x** (USD 1000 notional).
- Calendar `R` is stop-risk, not a dollar. Do not convert with `1R = $50`.
- UTC-day tape counts dollar hits at $$30 / $$50 / $$80 / $$100 / $$150 / $$200 / $$300 / $$350 / \$400: each cross up through that dollar on fill-true open-book uPnL. Own column per level; clocks in a hits table (Athens). After a lock flatten the counts freeze. Watcher ticks go to ClickHouse (`grade_b_book_samples` / `grade_b_book_hits`) for later replay.
- Stale demo snapshot (default >10 minutes) warns on the desk. It does **not** block new paper. Only a missing `demo_trader.json` with demo trading on blocks admission. Bitget demo stays one coin.

## Gates

- Score 45–74 inclusive, exclusive of 75
- Family is a trading family from `scripts/setup_families.py` (not `NO_SUPPORTED_SETUP`, not Hunter `BREAKOUT`)
- ARM quality gate: measured thesis, trigger, invalidation, TTL, chase, entry still available
- Ten-slot capacity, one position per coin, at most three in one direction and two per engine
- Shared flood guard on every send
