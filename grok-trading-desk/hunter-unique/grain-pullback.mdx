## What it is

`detect_grain_pullback` in `scripts/hunter_ripe_watch.py`. With-grain only. Last is beyond the swing stop, off the local extreme, not a chase.

## Clock and size

5m bars. Stop from a confirmed swing, padded to **0.4%** (`MIN_SL_PCT`) — no chip stops. TP from `levels_ok` with **R:R ≥ 1.5**.

## Radio

Ripe / TAPE cards when the hunter lane is on. Brain ignores these packed levels and rebuilds its own.

## Host

Evaluated every 60s on `grok-hunter-ripe.timer` and during extreme wakes. GP keeps priority over BREAKOUT.

## State / settle

Rows land in `hunter_ripe.json`. Paper / Unique settle is not automatic from a ripe pack.

## Gates

- `tone_24h_ok` and not `chase_blocked`
- Spread not too wide
- Majors: mid-range ban (`major_gp_mid_range_banned`)
- LONG: `swing < last < extreme`. SHORT: `extreme < last < swing`
- Pullback off the lookback extreme

## How Patrick uses it

Shortlist. Run brain or trade-check. Do not buy the ripe entry blindly.

## Decisions that still bind

GP / SR keep the name. BREAKOUT only packs when both fail. EXTREME only packs a symbol the other three left empty.
