## What it is

`detect_sweep_reclaim` in `scripts/hunter_ripe_watch.py`. Confirmed swings, then a later bar that sweeps and reclaims.

LONG: `bar.low < swing` and `bar.close > swing`. SHORT: `bar.high > swing` and `bar.close < swing`. Confirmation bar is not the sweep bar.

## Clock and size

5m. Same `MIN_SL_PCT` 0.4% and `RR_MIN` 1.5 as grain.

## Radio

TAPE / ripe when hunter radio is on. Not a brain TAKE by itself.

## Host

Same ripe timer as grain. If grain already packed the same swing, sweep is not duplicated.

## State / settle

`hunter_ripe.json`.

## Gates

Spread, 24h tone, chase block, `levels_ok`. With-grain only.

## How Patrick uses it

Liquidity sweep card. Confirm with brain or trade-check. Countertrend sweeps are structurally out of this packer.
