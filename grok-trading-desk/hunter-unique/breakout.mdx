## What it is

`detect_breakout` in `scripts/hunter_ripe_watch.py`. Needs at least 5 bars before the candidate break candle. Retest band around the anchored pre-break level: 0.15%.

This is **not** Grade B `BREAKOUT_CONFIRMATION` and **not** a Bitget demo family. Leftover Hunter BREAKOUT demo legs flatten.

## Clock and size

5m. Same SL floor 0.4% and R:R 1.5. Chase: refused when last already ran ≥ 1R past the trigger (trigger→SL).

## Radio

TAPE cards when hunter radio is on. Hunter telegram TAPE does **not** call demo ingest.

## Host

State: `/var/lib/skynet-market/hunter_breakout.state.json` (`skynet_hunter_breakout_state_v0`). Retained across ticks and restamped; never invent actionable.

## State / settle

Retained BREAKOUT / EXTREME rows stay for the live episode. A symbol flipping GP→BREAKOUT is a new pair and may POST.

## Gates

- GP and SR both failed to pack
- Close through prior high (long) / low (short), or wait-retest at the anchored level
- Bypass later-tick CHASE_LONG/CHASE_SHORT still-red/still-green only via the dedicated re-score path — keep spread, MIN_SL, RR

## How Patrick uses it
