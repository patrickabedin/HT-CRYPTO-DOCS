## What it is

`scripts/hunter_telegram.py` posts TAPE / UNIQUE TAKE cards (`SKYNET HUNTER *`) when the hunter lane is on. `scripts/hunter_telegram_settle.py` settles hunter paper outcomes for the PnL rollup.

`scripts/manual_take_projection.py` builds the shared $50-class level projection used on hunter / USELESS / telegram cards.

Unique paper lives in `hunter_brain.json` even when Telegram is muted. Tape paper lives in `hunter_telegram.json` and is a later book.
