## What it is

`scripts/hunter_telegram.py` posts TAPE / UNIQUE TAKE cards (`SKYNET HUNTER *`). `scripts/hunter_telegram_settle.py` settles hunter Telegram paper outcomes for the PnL rollup.

`scripts/manual_take_projection.py` builds the shared $50-class level projection used on hunter / USELESS / telegram cards.

## Clock and size

Chained after ripe / extreme / brain runs. Settle is not a quote stream.

## Radio

Lane `HUNTER`. Brain: TAKE only. Ripe TAPE is a shortlist card, not a brain TAKE. `editMessageText` does not consume the shared send budget.

## Host

Uses the same Telegram env as Grade B. State: `HUNTER_RIPE_PATH`, `HUNTER_SETTLE_PATH` under `/var/lib/skynet-market/`.

## State / settle

Dashboard groups: `HUNTER_UNIQUE` vs `HUNTER_TAPE`. EARLY settlements stay under their own engine names on the tape group — they are not relabelled GP/SR/BREAKOUT.

## Gates

Flood guard on every new send. Dedupe per engine rules. Do not publish EARLY as Hunter TAKE (`nudge_only` flag on those rows).

## How Patrick uses it

A TAPE card is a wake. A BRAIN TAKE is the ping worth trade-check. UNIQUE without brain structure is not the product.

## Decisions that still bind

Mute UNIQUE with `HUNTER_UNIQUE_TELEGRAM=0` if you need a quiet channel. Do not mute Grade B to punish hunter.
