Hunter Unique is the discretionary / named-engine book. The product is the **brain path**, not the ripe firehose.

<Warning>
  SHADOW / advisory. No exchange orders. Radio stays `GRADE_B,GRADE_A`. Lane `HUNTER` is muted. Brain Telegram is TAKE only and needs `HUNTER_UNIQUE_TELEGRAM=1` plus `HUNTER` on the radio — do not unmute either for a paper fix.
</Warning>

## What it is

Host ripe / engines cheaply shortlist. `scripts/hunter_brain.py` packs its own structure off the shared tape (WS quotes in `latest.json` + Valkey 5m). Engine entry/SL/TP on a ripe row are **ignored**. `trade_check` must return ENTER before a brain TAKE.

Named Unique packers: `GRAIN_PULLBACK`, `SWEEP_RECLAIM`, `EXTREME_MOMENTUM`. Unique does **not** pack `BREAKOUT` — that leftover packer stays on [Hunter Tape](/grok-trading-desk/hunter-tape). EARLY names that appear on ripe rows belong to the [EARLY book](/grok-trading-desk/early).

## Clock and size

5m chart. Brain timer 60s. Projection on hunter-setup cards: $50 @ 20x (label). Do not treat that as a live order.

Dedup key is `symbol|engine|side|episode`. Episode is the grain alert (`STATE@last_market_alert_ts`). Empty episode was an all-time one-slot key and undercounted reprints.

## Radio vs paper

- Brain card: `SKYNET HUNTER BRAIN` — TAKE only when unmuted
- UNIQUE Telegram also needs `HUNTER_UNIQUE_TELEGRAM=1` **and** `TELEGRAM_ALLOW` to include `HUNTER`
- WAIT / SKIP / chop / no grain → silent
- **Mute is chat, not calendar.** TAKEs still persist to `hunter_brain.json` so Unique paper can settle. First `entry_ts` / `updated_ts` is frozen so 24h max-hold cannot be reset by a reprint.

## Host

`grok-hunter-brain.timer` (60s). State: `/var/lib/skynet-market/hunter_brain.json`. Leave `grok-hunter-ripe.timer` running if you still want the raw shortlist.
