## What it is

Collects and settles. Does **not** invent win rates. Question for later: are rejects below Grade A — especially scores 20–44 — profitable on a frozen plan? Does existing Grade B (45–74) outperform those bands?

Scripts: `triggered_cf_book.py`, `triggered_cf_scoreboard.py`.

## Clock and size

Armed watcher may settle due plans after the trigger tick. Scoreboard has no Telegram.

## State / settle

Three datasets — do not mix:

| Family | Population | Tables | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | WATCHING / ARMED that never triggered | `opportunity_quality_labels` (`GROK_OQ_V0`) | Forward return / MFE / MAE. Not win-rate |
| B | Genuine ARMED → TRIGGERED | `triggered_cf_plans` + `triggered_cf_outcomes` (`GROK_TRIGGERED_CF_V0`) | Frozen plan + NET R after costs |
| C | Live Grade A | `grok_plan_outcomes` (`GROK_PLAN_C_V0`) | Production threshold 75 |

SKYNET reconstructed plans (`GROK_RECONSTRUCTED_V0`) are **not** original SKYNET and are not family B.

## Gates

Freeze **before** Qualifier / Risk / Chief. Future candles dropped. Missing structure → honest `VOID` / `INSUFFICIENT_PLAN`. Every score gets a family-B row or VOID.

Do not compute band win-rate from family A. Do not fold `INSUFFICIENT_PLAN` scout-rejects into family B.

Existing Grade B shadow tracking is **not rebuilt**. SCORE_65_74 joins `grade_b_shadow` on `candidate_id`.
