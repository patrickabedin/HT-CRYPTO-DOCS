The live Expandables calendar banks `EXPAND_STOP` and then sits. The what-if book asks what the day would have printed if that sit never happened.

<Warning>
  Paper only. Never demo-attaches. Never places, cancels, or withdraws orders.
</Warning>

## What it is

`scripts/whatif_book.py` keeps lock-flatten tickets on per-ticket exits (TP / SL / STALL / MAX_HOLD / TICKET_TRAIL). While live is paused, the armed watcher still admits TREND / RECLAIM / EXTREME into this book (cap 3, one coin).

Dashboard `?g=EXPANDABLES` shows:

1. **Expandables paper** — live locked seats (`grade_b_open` filtered to the three engines). Empty after flatten is correct.
2. **What-if paper** — still-open `WHATIF_OPEN` marks next to that live book. Do not hide an open because the quote file missed a last.
3. Live calendar tickets under `#day-trades`. What-if tickets under `#whatif-trades`.

TREND / RECLAIM / EXTREME belong on this chip even if a leftover host row still says `group=GRADE_B` (pre-split RECLAIM). `lane_for` and `whatifDeskGroup` route by engine.

## Clock and size

Same USD 50 at 20x. What-if TP1 / STOP bank at the **card** level, not the overshoot last. `parse_dt` must accept unix ms or TP1 / MAX_HOLD never fire.

## Radio

None.

## Host

`desk_pnl.py` adopts live lock outcomes, cycles both lanes, and POSTs `whatif_rows` with the compact ingest.

## State / settle

- `expand_whatif.json`
- `grade_b_whatif.json` (Grade B lane only)

## Gates

- `WHATIF_OPEN` stays visible with `usd` null (unpriced mark). Cell **o** count is opens; **n** is natural closes.
- Day-lock outcomes (`EXPAND_STOP`, `DAY_STOP`, `BOOK_LOCK`, `BOOK_GIVEBACK`, `EXPAND_BANK`) drop from the what-if calendar.
- Do not count open marks as wins or losses.

## How Patrick uses it

After flatten the live book says none open. Scroll stays on `#day-trades` / `#whatif-trades` after a day click. What-if paper at the top is the still-running no-lock seats.
