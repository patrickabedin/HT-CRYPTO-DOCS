## TP1 peel vs hold the book

Expandables **peels**. First TP1 does not flatten the other seats. That is coded intent ("green days keep printing"), not a Grade B lock bug.

| Rule | 5d live-ticket replay |
| --- | --- |
| First winner (TP1 / giveback / trail) sits | **-$74** (17 Sep dies at RAY +$12) |
| First TP1 >= $30 sits | -$53 (17 Sep +$29 at ONE +$73; 18 Sep +$35 at WLD) |
| No refill after first TP1 | -$72 |

Peel is how 17 Sep printed three ONE tickets ($11 trail, +$73, +$128 = **+$212**). Holding the book like Grade B armed-TP1 `BOOK_LOCK` would need a new product and a new child page. Do not silent-copy it.
