## What it is

Cap **3** open seats, one coin. The same engine may stack all three seats. Combined UTC-day cash (realized + open) at or below minus USD 50 flattens and sits until the next UTC day (`EXPAND_STOP`). There is no plus-USD-50 profit bank.

Live paper is empty after that sit. That is honest. The no-lock book keeps those seats running — see [What-if book](/grok-trading-desk/expandables/whatif).

Leftover Grade B Bitget fills of TREND / RECLAIM / EXTREME stay on the Grade B chip. They do not write this calendar.

## Clock and size

USD 50 margin at 20x. Calendar cell is banked cash (closed tickets + flatten remainder). The tape keeps the open-book high.
