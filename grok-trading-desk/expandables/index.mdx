## What it is

`scripts/expandables.py` + `scripts/expandables_portfolio.py`. Own state and pause. Green days keep printing unless `EXPAND_BANK_USDT>0` (default **off**). Combined UTC-day cash (realized + open) ≤ **−$50** flattens and sits until the next UTC day (`EXPAND_STOP`).

Per-ticket trail still arms at +$50 and gives back 25% of that seat (`TICKET_TRAIL`). Bitget demo places a 2.5% callback net after the same arm. See [Ticket trail](/grok-trading-desk/grade-b/ticket-trail).
