## What it is

`scripts/early_impulse.py` (`ENGINE_EARLY_PUMP`). Join as the move starts.

A move is BUILDING when all four line up on the **5m** clock (not 1m):

1. Range expands: this 5m (high-low)/low > previous 5m range × **1.15**
2. Volume expands: this 5m quote volume > previous × **1.25**
3. New extreme: last > previous 5m high
4. Takers agree if the quote has them; otherwise green bar (close > open)

## Clock and size

Need at least 3 completed-or-current 5m bars. Previous 5m already expanded the same way → late / chase → skip.

## Radio

No Hunter TAKE. Independent paper via [trade book](/grok-trading-desk/early/trade-book).

## Host

Detected during ripe scan; settled by `grok-early-trade-book.timer`.

## State / settle

`EARLY_TRADE_SETTLE_PATH`. Health cadence 60s.

## Gates

Both range **and** volume must expand. One-axis explosion is not PUMP (and is NO SIGNAL for GRIND).

## How Patrick uses it

First expanding bar only. Do not chase bar 3.

## Decisions that still bind
