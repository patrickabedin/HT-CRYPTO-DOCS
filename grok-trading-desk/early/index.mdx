EARLY_PUMP, EARLY_DUMP, and EARLY_GRIND are **independent trade-producing SHADOW engines**. Detector rows still carry `nudge_only` so Hunter Telegram cannot publish them as Hunter TAKE cards.

<Warning>
  SHADOW. No Telegram TAKE. No Bitget orders. No Wave 9.
</Warning>

## What it is

Detectors run on the 5m clock inside ripe scan. `scripts/early_trade_book.py` consumes actionable signals and keeps one paper trade per engine/symbol/side/5m signal bar. Settlements use the shared 5m cache and canonical SHADOW settlement rules.

Dashboard: `HUNTER_TAPE` group, **own engine names**. Do not relabel as BREAKOUT / GP / SR.

## Clock and size

60s `grok-early-trade-book.timer`. 5m bars. Simulated $50 @ 20x on the desk unless a page says otherwise.

## Radio

None for TAKE. Health distinguishes ALIVE-but-quiet from STALE/DEAD.
