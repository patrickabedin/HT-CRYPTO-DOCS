## What it is

`scripts/early_trade_book.py`. Consumes actionable EARLY signals. Settles entry/SL/TP from the shared 5m bar cache.

## Clock and size

`grok-early-trade-book.timer` 60s. Dashboard early table: `web/app/early-table.tsx`.

## Radio

None.

## Host

Settle path env `EARLY_TRADE_SETTLE_PATH` under `/var/lib/skynet-market/`.

## State / settle

One paper trade per engine/symbol/side/5m signal bar. Added to `HUNTER_TAPE` under EARLY_* names.

## Gates

No Telegram TAKE, no Bitget placement. Health required to interpret zero trades.

## How Patrick uses it

Read the early table and engine health together.

## Decisions that still bind

Do not relabel EARLY as another Hunter engine. Do not claim pre-ADR history as this book's track record.
