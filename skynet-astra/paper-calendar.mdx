The desk snapshot book stays tagged `ASTRA_STRUCTURE_V1`. Individual paper rows carry the scanner that emitted them: Structure, Trend Pullback, Crowded Fade, or BTC Impulse.

## View PnL

Each engine card opens a drawer over that engine's paper only. Calendar cells, closed/open counts, and after-fee totals come from:

1. The snapshot `engines` rollup published by the worker (all persisted trades, Athens close date).
2. If that rollup is missing (older snapshot), the drawer derives days from the tagged trades still on the snapshot.

The book-level `engine_id` is not a filter. Treating a Trend/Fade/Impulse drawer as empty because the book says Structure is a bug, not a no-data state.

## What is not on this calendar

- Historical SOL scorecards in `/api/history`
- Exchange fills or demo-account attribution
- Invented exits for unmeasured candidates

Unknown outcomes stay unknown. A day with no measured close shows an em dash, not zero profit.

## Policy

Sampled-quote paper: 50 USDT margin, 10x, 500 USDT notional. Assumed 6 bps fee and 2 bps adverse slippage each side. Funding excluded. No live order authority.
