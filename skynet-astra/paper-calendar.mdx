The desk snapshot book stays tagged `ASTRA_STRUCTURE_V1`. Individual paper rows carry the scanner that emitted them.

## View PnL

Each engine card opens that engine's paper only. Calendar cells come from the worker `engines` rollup, not the book-level `engine_id`.

## BTC Impulse

`ASTRA_BTC_IMPULSE_V1` does not copy SOL's too-early 22-trade slate onto this calendar. Empty cells mean no prospective ASTRA paper yet.

The gate is BTC ±2% on 48 contiguous 5m bars (SOL's 4h window) when the cache has them, else 24×5m. The drawer shows the observed BTC return. Paper starts only after that window opens and an alt passes residual gates. Historical SOL impulse stays in `/api/history`.

## What is not on this calendar

- Historical SOL scorecards
- Exchange fills or demo-account attribution
- Invented exits for unmeasured candidates

Unknown outcomes stay unknown. A day with no measured close shows an em dash, not zero profit.
